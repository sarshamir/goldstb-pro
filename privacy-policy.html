GOLDSTB PRO Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

GOLDSTB PRO is an independent media-player client. It does not provide television channels, streams, playlists, portals, or content subscriptions.

Information handled by the app

The portal URL and MAC/account details entered by the user are held in memory while the app is being used. They are transmitted only to the portal selected by the user so that the requested connection and playback functions can operate. This version does not intentionally persist those portal credentials after the app process ends.

Favourites and acceptance of the legal notice are stored locally on the user's device. They are not transmitted to the publisher. The app does not include publisher-operated analytics or advertising SDKs and the publisher does not sell personal information collected through the app.

Third-party portals

A portal selected by the user may independently receive account, device, network, and usage information. That provider's own privacy policy and practices apply. GOLDSTB PRO does not control third-party portals.

Data removal

Users can remove locally stored application data through Android system settings or by uninstalling the app.
